What is VapoCept used for?

VapoCept is marketed as a dietary supplement for everyday cognitive wellness, including memory, focus and mental clarity. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. See about VapoCept.

How do I take VapoCept?

The formula includes green tea leaf extract, which can contain caffeine, and the exact caffeine content is not stated. Ask the manufacturer or your doctor if you need to avoid caffeine.

Is it safe with my medicines?

Ask your doctor or pharmacist first, especially about blood thinners. See Ginkgo and blood thinners.

No. It is a dietary supplement and does not treat memory loss, dementia or any condition. See what memory supplements cannot do.

Anyone under 18, pregnant or nursing, and anyone with a bleeding disorder or planned surgery who has not asked a doctor first.

No timeline is promised and results vary. Judge any routine over weeks, beside sleep, meals and movement.
